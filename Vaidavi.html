Research Direction

A Focus on Pharmacognosy

Pharmacognosy is the direction you're working toward — the study of medicines derived from natural sources: plants, minerals, and other natural substances.

The route there runs through the training already completed: a science background, five years of BAMS at R. A. Podar Ayurved Medical College, and the internship year just finished.

The research interest runs deeper still — pearl-based medicine research (Moti Pishti), the classical pearl preparation, approached with a modern research eye.

Medicine that nature already wrote — researched, one pearl at a time.
