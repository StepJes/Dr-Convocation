CONTENT ACCURACY
================

Treat Instagram content as supplementary evidence, not as permission to invent credentials.

For example:

If her Reels discuss a particular medical topic, you may say:

"Dr. Esha creates educational content around [verified topic]."

Do NOT turn that into:

"Dr. Esha specializes in [topic]."

Those are completely different claims.

A Reel about PCOS does not automatically mean she specializes in PCOS.

A Reel about pregnancy does not automatically mean she is an obstetrician.

A Reel about skincare does not automatically mean she specializes in dermatology.

A Reel about nutrition does not automatically make her a nutritionist.

Be extremely careful about this distinction.
